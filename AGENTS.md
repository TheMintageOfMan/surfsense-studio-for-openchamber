# Project guidance

Read `SPEC.md`, `README.md`, and `nextsteps.md` first; `nextsteps.md` holds the current plan and working setup. This is an independently distributed, optional extension, not part of OpenChamber's setup. Preserve `windows-11-x64/` and `linux-fedora-amd64/`; edit shared source in `shared/` and rebuild with `npm run build`. Nine formats are implemented; Podcast, Image, and Infographic appear as disabled grid tiles with no implementation. Document formats (Word, Slides, Spreadsheet, PDF) are validated in `shared/common/documents.mjs` and built by Node-only JavaScript builders in `shared/service/builders/`: no self-packaged Python, and the panel never imports the builders. Every bundled package and font needs license text; the build fails otherwise (vendored notices live in `shared/licenses/`). Password-protected, remote, and relay hosts are out of scope. Distinguish controlled tests from live model verification and untested platform builds. The panel targets non-technical users (age 10 and up): pick sources, tap a tile, open, and save, with defaults chosen automatically and technical detail only under **About this**. Quiet recovery is limited to the retry budget in `shared/service/jobs.mjs`: three text-model calls per request, every one recorded. Do not weaken authentication, truncate sources, or copy generated-code execution from upstream. Keep scratch work under `temp/` and generated history under `.studio/`, both out of commits.
